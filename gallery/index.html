Tattoo Gallery

A permanent record of every tattoo you have completed. Each card shows the design name, the client who requested it, your grade (S through F), and the date it was finished.

Grading System

Tattoos are graded S, A, B, C, D, or F based on the precision and smoothness of your freehand trace. Higher grades earn more XP and larger client tips.

Your Portfolio

As your skills improve, watch your gallery fill with higher-grade work. The gallery is a visual history of your progression from apprentice to master.
